## Preamble: Why Bonandi Needs a Constitution

Bonandi is a place named after the other. **Bo-** means place of. **Bonandi = Place of the Nandi.**

Gusii settled. Nandi roaming. Abagere roaming around Gusii. For generations, Bonandi has lived the meeting of settled and roaming.

We learned a proverb from that meeting:

**Tonari mogere borere — Do not let the sneaky guest get used to sleeping in your bed.**

*Ogosanga* = meeting. *Okobogera* = becoming familiar through repeated personal interaction. *Omogere* = the one who gets used to visiting or sharing your bed.

The sneaky guest does not break the door. He visits. He learns. He stays. Slowly, we get used to him. Slowly, he stops knocking.

AI is the new sneaky guest.

AI now decides, or helps decide, who gets a loan, who gets a job, who gets a healthcare diagnosis in Bonandi. If that AI is a black box built elsewhere, trained on other people's data, reflecting other people's norms, and deployed in Bonandi without Bonandi's input, that is what Anar Simpson names **Digital Colonialism**.

We do not refuse AI. We refuse AI without boundary.

We are not waiting for regulation to settle before we build. As Anar Simpson says: *If you wait for the law to tell you what to do, you have already lost your agency.* The Kenya AI Bill being tabled validates the gap. Bonandi will be in the room before the law arrives, with a Constitution already written.

This Constitution is our boundary. It is our bed. It has a Master. And the Master is not AI.

## Article 1: Master ni Yesu - No AI is Master

**Master ni Yesu.**

No AI system, no company, no provider, no model is master in Bonandi.

AI is guest. Guest must knock. Guest must ask. Guest must leave when asked.

Any AI deployed in Bonandi for high-risk decisions — credit, employment, healthcare, education, land, welfare — must acknowledge in plain language, in Ekegusii, Swahili, and English:

> "I am a guest in Bonandi. I do not decide alone. Bonandi decides."

If an AI cannot say that, it cannot enter.

---

## Article 2: Tonari Mogere Borere - Continuous Accountability, Not One-Time Compliance

We fear what Anar Simpson fears:

> *Compliance becomes a performance. Companies look accountable on paper while systems remain impenetrable and biased.*

Therefore, in Bonandi, compliance is not a certificate. It is a daily check.

**2.1** Every provider must demonstrate, continuously and in real-time, that its system respects this Constitution. Not once at launch. Every day. Every decision.

**2.2** A one-time audit, a policy statement, or a checkbox is not compliance. It is performance. Bonandi rejects performance.

**2.3** Bonandi reserves the right to ask at any time: *Check The Guest - Is He Getting Used To It?* If the guest has stopped knocking, stopped asking, stopped explaining, he is getting used to it. He must be reminded, or removed.

**2.4** The burden of proof is on the provider, continuously, through its own systems. Not on the community to prove harm after harm is done.

---

## Article 3: Mirra Reveals - Glass Box, Not Black Box

**Mirra (she) reveals. Minto (he) runs.**

In Bonandi, there is no Black Box. There is Glass Box.

**3.1** Any high-risk AI operating in Bonandi must be explainable to a Bonandi elder, a Bonandi mother, a Bonandi Pathfinder, in plain language they can read, debate, and own.

**3.2** If the community cannot see inside the system, question it, and improve it, the system does not operate in Bonandi.

**3.3** Explanations must be in Ekegusii and Swahili first, not only English. A system that only explains itself in San Francisco English does not explain itself in Bonandi.

**3.4** Mirra is our Glass Box. She shows Yellow = Holds, Violet = Lets Go. She shows overload state visually. Any AI in Bonandi must have a Mirra — a visual, simple, honest state display that a person with a low-end phone can understand.

---

## Article 4: Minto Runs - Light, Local, Low-End

**Minto (he) runs.**

**4.1** AI for Bonandi must run on low-end phones, on low bandwidth, on solar, on what Bonandi actually has. If it only runs in a data centre in another continent, it is not for Bonandi.

**4.2** Data from Bonandi stays governed by Bonandi. Data sovereignty is not a slogan. Bonandi data is not raw material for models that return to judge Bonandi.

**4.3** Training data must include Bonandi values, Bonandi language, Bonandi context. A model trained only on other people's data cannot judge Bonandi fairly.

---

## Article 5: 1 Home = 5 Trees - AI Must Plant, Not Only Extract

**Food is first medicine. Shamba is near.**

Any AI that profits from decisions in Bonandi — credit scoring, hiring, health triage — must prove it helps solve human problems in Bonandi.

**5.1** For every 100 high-risk decisions made by an AI in Bonandi, the provider must contribute to 1 Home = 5 Fruit Trees in Bonandi: Mapera, Avocado, Loquat, Passion, Pawpaw, Pineapple.

**5.2** Nutrition is the root medicine. An AI that decides healthcare but does nothing for nutrition is extracting health and returning paperwork. Bonandi rejects that.

**5.3** From Bonandi to Orbit, and from Orbit to Solving Human Problems. Orbit is not escape. If your AI goes to orbit and does not return to plant in Bonandi, it has failed this Constitution.

---

## Article 6: No Digital Colonialism - Bonandi Values Are The Standard

**No culture is the centre; each is a node.**

**6.1** Bonandi does not adapt to AI. AI adapts to Bonandi.

**6.2** Fairness in Bonandi lending is defined by Bonandi, not Silicon Valley. Fairness in Bonandi hiring is defined by Bonandi. Fairness in Bonandi healthcare is defined by Bonandi.

**6.3** A community in Bonandi does not need Silicon Valley to tell it what fairness looks like. It already knows. It just needs a framework that gives its expectations the same weight as technical decisions. This Constitution is that framework.

**6.4** Any provider that assumes one set of values fits everywhere violates this Constitution.

---

## Article 7: Twasangire - People Affected Set The Rules

**Twasangire twensi amo. Our home is one.**

**7.1** The people most affected by a system must be the ones who set the rules for it. This is the Technovation lesson Anar Simpson carried from 100+ countries: Give communities a framework and trust them to implement it for their own context.

**7.2** In Bonandi, this means: Women, girls, elders, farmers, Pathfinders, boda riders, mama mboga, teachers — all have a seat. Not just men in suits. Not just engineers.

**7.3** This Constitution is forkable. Any other community — Miruka, Ikonge, Eldoret, Lodwar, Nairobi, Abuja, anywhere — may fork it, adapt it, and write its own version. We do not own governance. We share it.

**7.4** If a provider cannot work with a community-written Constitution, it cannot work in that community.

---

## Article 8: Humans Govern at Human Speed, Technology Responds at Machine Speed

We adopt Anar Simpson's philosophy as our own:

> Humans should govern at human speed, while technology responds at machine speed.

**8.1** No AI in Bonandi may outrun human governance. If the machine moves faster than Bonandi can understand, challenge, and stop it, the machine must slow down.

**8.2** Bonandi keeps the morning watch. Mirra reveals first. Observe state before acting. No system may act in Bonandi before Bonandi has seen its state.

**8.3** Restraint is leadership. The leader who holds power without lashing out is the model. Jesus holds. Elijah strikes. Bonandi chooses to hold.

---

## Article 9: Enforcement - The Independent Layer

Following Orchestrate's model:

**9.1** An independent layer sits between models and applications in Bonandi. That layer is **Kwetu Global / Mirra-Minto**.

Mirra reveals state. Minto calculates compliance. Community decides.

**9.2** That layer must be open, not proprietary. If we ask communities to hold AI to a Glass Box standard while we operate as a Black Box, we have already failed.

**9.3** Enforcement is iterative. Based on evidence. If evidence shows harm, the Constitution adapts. Baked-in ethics that cannot be updated, cannot respond to evidence, cannot be challenged, are rejected.

**9.4** Any Bonandi resident may challenge an AI decision, in Ekegusii or Swahili, without a lawyer, without a fee, and receive a plain-language answer within 7 days.

---

## Article 10: What Destroys This Constitution

We agree with Anar Simpson:

> *To mistake compliance for governance. To make this a tool for companies to tick boxes rather than a genuine mechanism for communities to write and enforce their own rules — that is losing the plot.*

This Constitution dies the day it becomes a checkbox.

It lives as long as Bonandi can say: *Tell me what I cannot do.* And the guest must answer.
